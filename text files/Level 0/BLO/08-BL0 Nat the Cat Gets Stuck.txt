Nat the Cat Gets Stuck 
 
Nat the Cat likes to climb trees. 
Nat likes to climb way up high. 
Nat tries to climb down. 
Oh, no! Nat is stuck. 
Big Pig runs to get help. 
Big Pig runs back to the tree. 
"Here we go!" says Big Pig. 
Everyone holds on. 
Nat jumps. 
Boing! Boing! 
Now Nat the Cat will stay with her friends!
