At Evening 
 
In the evening Grasshopper walked slowly along 
the road. 
The sun was going down. 
The world was soft and quiet. 
Grasshopper heard a loud sound. 
ZOOM! Grasshopper heard another noise. 
ZOOOM! 
He saw two dragonflies in the air. 
“Poor Grasshopper,” said the dragonflies. 
“We are flying fast. You are only walking. 
That is very sad.” 
“It is not sad,” said Grasshopper. 
“I like to walk.” 
 
 
The dragonflies flew over Grasshopper’s head. 
“We can see so many things from up here,” said 
the dragonflies. 
“All you can see is that road.” 
“I like this road,” said Grasshopper. 
“And I can see flowers growing along the side of 
the road.” 
 
 
“We are zipping and zooming,” said the first 
dragonfly. 
“We do not have time to look at flowers.” 
 
“I can see leaves moving in the trees,” said 
Grasshopper. 
“We are looping and spinning,” said the second 
dragonfly. 
“We do not have time to look at leaves.” 
“I can see the sunset over the mountains,” said 
Grasshopper. 
“What sunset? 
What mountains?” asked the dragonflies. 
“We are diving and dipping. 
 
There is no time to look at sunsets and moun- 
tains.” 
 

 
ZOOOOM! 
The two dragonflies raced across the sky. 
Soon they were gone. 
The world was quiet again. 
The sky became dark. Grasshopper watched the 
moon rising over the land. 
He watched the stars come out. 
He was happy to be walking slowly down the 
road.
